03 · Customer reference check

Why customer-reference integrity is blocked

The latest direct-ID comparison found 48,461 unresolved sales rows across 44,753 distinct nonblank IDs. These are exploratory counts—not proof that the records are invalid.

  • The lab ETL script documents a join from distributor sales to Customer Master on `customer_id`, then selects `customer_key`.
  • Read-only comparison of supplied input files found no shared nonblank sales and master `customer_id` values.
  • All 50,000 distinct sales policy numbers appear in the Customer Master policy-number set, but the available ETL script does not use policy number for this join.
  • No separate approved crosswalk or instruction to substitute policy number was found.
Decision required: Data owner/mentor to confirm the authoritative key, active-customer rule, and treatment of duplicate IDs. Do not mark the check verified or change the join based on overlap alone.

Supporting analysis: WHY_STG_CUSTOMER_REF_IS_BLOCKED.md. The validation currently emits BLOCKED by design until the mapping is approved.

05 · Recommended decisions and next steps

Resolve assumptions, then retest before sign-off

  4. Disposition source defects: confirm whether blank sales customer IDs are allowed and whether duplicate master IDs are valid.
  5. Complete remaining layers: obtain aggregate/report requirements and dashboard evidence, or formal agreement they are out of scope.
